====== The Dragon's Cave ======
Legend says a chest of gold is hidden in the dragon's cave.
Torch in hand, you stand at the entrance.

(Your torch will last 12 more steps)
You are at the entrance. It is dark. A cold wind blows.
1. Go into the cave   2. Forget it, go home
> 1

(Your torch will last 11 more steps)
You reach a fork. On the left is a small wooden door.
From the right comes the sound of snoring.
1. Open the door   2. Go right   3. Back to the entrance
> 2

(Your torch will last 10 more steps)
The dragon's lair! A big red dragon is asleep on a chest.
1. Open the chest   2. Wake the dragon   3. Tiptoe back
> 2
You poke the dragon's nose. It opens one eye.
The dragon says: "I only like people who are good at math.
What is 1 + 2 + ... + 100? You get 3 tries."
Try 1 > 5000
The dragon says: "Too small."
Try 2 > 5050
The dragon says: "Not bad!"
It moves aside and lets you take the chest.

*** You win! You took 3 steps. ***
